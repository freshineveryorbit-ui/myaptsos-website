The Problem

The Gap Between Councillors and Citizens

Ward councillors in Telangana receive hundreds of complaints through phone calls, personal visits, and unstructured WhatsApp messages every week. There is no system — complaints get lost, timelines are invisible, and citizens feel unheard. MyCivicOS turns every ward councillor into a digitally-empowered public servant — with zero behavior change required from citizens.

How It Works

From Complaint to Resolution — In Minutes

1

Citizen Scans Ward QR

Unique QR code on hoardings, pamphlets, or ID cards. WhatsApp opens automatically.

2

Files Complaint via WhatsApp

Selects category (Roads / Water / Sanitation), describes issue, optionally uploads a photo. Gets Complaint ID instantly.

3

Councillor Gets Instant Alert

Dashboard notifies councillor. Field team assigned with SLA timer. Citizen tracked automatically.

4

Resolved with Proof

Field team uploads photo evidence. Citizen receives WhatsApp confirmation. Audit trail complete.

MyLifeOS brings everything into one structured ecosystem, designed specifically around how human life actually progresses through stages.

Unlike generic productivity apps built for Western markets, MyLifeOS is designed for Indian life — joint families, local education systems, regional career paths, vernacular languages, and community-based living.
