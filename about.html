About MyOS Group

Digital operating systems for how India actually lives — built from Hyderabad, for the country's communities.

Who We Are

Building the Infrastructure Layer for India's Communities

MyOS Group develops digital platforms for citizens, communities, and individuals — purpose-built for how India actually organises its everyday life. Instead of adapting generic, foreign software, we design from first principles around UPI payments, WhatsApp-native workflows, RWA governance, ward-level civic structures, and the realities of Indian households.

Every product we build shares the same philosophy: meet people where they already are, remove friction from everyday processes, and give communities a structured, transparent way to run themselves.

Our Products

Three Operating Systems, One Ecosystem

🏢

MyAptsOS — Apartment Society OS

The operating system for Indian RWA-governed gated communities — UPI maintenance payments, complaint management, gate & visitor logs, and a hyperlocal vendor marketplace. Live in Hyderabad.

🏛️

MyCivicOS — Civic Governance OS

A WhatsApp-native complaint management platform connecting ward councillors and citizens across Telangana's ULBs, with QR-code onboarding and real-time dashboards.

🌱

MyLifeOS — Personal Life OS

An intelligent life-management platform that adapts to your age and life stage — from education to retirement — bringing tasks, habits, goals, and finances into one place.
